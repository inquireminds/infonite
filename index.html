Routines & Digital Norms Learning Frameworks & UDL Long Range Curriculum Plans SDG & Critical Thinking

School Routines & Digital Citizenship

Supporting smooth transitions, responsibility, and safe technology use.

Locker Access Routines

Lockers are accessed during designated times: morning entry, before/after recess, and dismissal. Keeping backpacks and outerwear in lockers keeps classrooms safe, spacious, and focused on learning.

Prepared to Learn Daily

Students are expected to arrive in class on time with basic supplies: pens/pencils, paper, notebooks, and a fully charged TDSB Chromebook ready for instructional tasks.

Digital Citizenship & TDSB Online Safety Norms

In alignment with TDSB guidelines and Common Sense Media digital principles, we teach students to maintain a safe, positive, and responsible online presence:

👣 Digital Footprint: Reminding students that online actions leave permanent records.
🔒 Privacy & Safety: Keeping personal passwords, information, and data secure.
⚖️ Device Balance: Encouraging healthy screen-time habits at school and home.

Pedagogy & Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

Ensuring learning is accessible, engaging, and meaningful for every student.

BTC

Building Thinking Classrooms

Students collaborate in visibly random groups at vertical whiteboards to solve complex problems, share strategies, and explain their mathematical thinking.

Inquiry

Inquiry-Driven Learning

Students investigate big real-world questions, develop research skills, analyze evidence, and communicate findings across science, history, and language.

UDL

Universal Design for Learning (Making Learning Accessible)

Universal Design for Learning ensures every student can access the curriculum successfully. We provide multiple entry points, flexible options for demonstrating understanding (writing, oral presentations, visual models, digital tools), and supportive learning scaffolds tailored to individual student needs.

Promoting Critical Thinking at Home

Using Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and Fermi problems to spark mathematical and scientific reasoning.

Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

In Science, students explore Water Systems and Heat in the Environment. These global goal prompts build environmental awareness and real-world connections:

  • 🌊 "How does water accessibility impact local ecosystems and communities globally?" (SDG 6)
  • 🔥 "What household modifications reduce thermal energy loss during winter?" (SDG 7 & 13)

Fermi Problem Estimation

Fermi activities encourage estimation, breaking big problems into smaller steps, and building numerical intuition:

  • 💡 "How many liters of water are used by David Lewis P.S. students over a school year?"
  • 💡 "If all student desks in Grade 7/8 were lined up, how far down Fundy Bay Blvd would they reach?"
